📋 Postseason Bracket Rules & Format

  • Wild Card Round: Best-of-3 series hosted entirely at the home stadium of the higher seed.
  • Division Series (DS): Best-of-5 series using a 2-2-1 home-field advantage format.
  • League Championship Series (LCS): Best-of-7 series using a 2-3-2 format to crown the AL and NL Pennant winners.
  • World Series: Best-of-7 fall classic hosted by the pennant winner with superior regular season win percentage.
